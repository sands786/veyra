## Evidence boundary

| Surface | What the viewer sees | What is not claimed |
|---|---|---|
| Demo Payroll | Local state visibly advances to **Shielded** and receipt **Submitted**. | A Mainnet settlement or recipient private-note delivery. |
| Operations, Treasury, Claims | Recovered local schedule, policy, and redemption states. | Production execution or public roster disclosure. |
| Selective Proof | Aggregate disclosure with a **Hidden** roster. | A proof receipt from the simulated interaction. |
| Private Markets | Public signal/private-actor framing and execution gate. | Completed trade or market settlement. |
| Launchpad and Proof | **Allocation Reserved** and **Proof Published** states in a clearly labeled guided simulation. | Capital movement, project release, or real onchain proof publication. |
| Mainnet boundary | Wallet approval, returned hash, confirmed receipt are named as the production standard. | Any generic invocation fallback, public transfer, or unverified transaction. |

## Caption guardrail

The captioned export never shows more than **one short line** at a time. It uses a lower-left safe area, is absent from the opening and ending teaser scenes, and is timed not to cover active product controls. It is a chapter identifier, not a full narration transcript.

The official hub wording is **“a 3-minute demo video”**; it does not publish a separate numeric minimum or maximum.
