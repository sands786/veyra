## Voice and edit direction

The final narration target is **105–112 seconds** at a natural, decisive pace, with short intentional pauses around the Mainnet boundary. Music enters with the teaser, ducks under narration, and resolves during the final teaser. The clean master carries no captions; an optional accessibility version can use compact, lower-left subtitles only during the live-product middle and must never overlap a control.

Each live sequence begins from an actual route and contains at least one meaningful browser action. The cursor is enlarged and smoothed in post-production, but the application itself is never replaced with screenshot motion. Hard cuts remove waiting time. Crossfades are reserved for route changes; a restrained 130–140% eased focus crop may be used only to isolate the clicked control.

## Acceptance criteria

The delivery must be **at or below 120 seconds**, 1920×1080, 60 fps, H.264, and approximately 8–12 Mbps. The final quality review must inspect frames from the opening, each of the four required product surfaces, the Mainnet boundary, and the final teaser. It must reject any cut that has browser chrome, desktop clutter, caption collisions, static screenshot panning, a fake Mainnet outcome, or unresolved export artefacts.
